🔄 How It Functions

About Report & Register

ObjectFinder connects campus members who lost belongings with those who find them. Here is how reporting and registering works:

📋

1. Report Lost Item

If you have misplaced or lost any personal property on campus — like your phone, wallet, ID card, bag, books, or lab coat — submit a lost item report immediately.

✓ Provide your department (RBMI, RBCET, RBTTI, RBCP, RBCN)
✓ Specify your year (if student) or position (faculty, HOD, staff)
✓ Describe item details (color, shape, brand, where lost)
✓ Your report is instantly published to "View Reported Items"
Go to Report Item Form →
✏️

2. Register Found Item

Found an unattended item anywhere on the campus? Help fellow students and faculty by registering the item so the rightful owner can verify and claim it.

✓ Register found object with location (e.g. Canteen, CS Lab, Library)
✓ Provide your contact number or security office drop point
✓ Add distinguishing features (shape, color, condition)
✓ Allows verified campus members to reclaim their belongings
Go to Register Item Form →

About ObjectFinder

ObjectFinder is a student-centered digital initiative designed specifically for the Rakshpal Bahadur Group of Institutions (RBMI) campus. Losing personal property during college lectures, labs, sports, or canteen breaks causes unnecessary stress.

Through ObjectFinder, students, faculty, and administrative staff across all college departments can collaborate to log lost items and return found property transparently.
